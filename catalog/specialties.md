# Browse by specialty

The catalogues are organized by clinical or technical use. Some resources support several specialties. A catalogue row is one source record; cross-specialty links do not create a new dataset.

| Specialty or area | Start here | Current coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Oral medicine and oral mucosal disease | [Oral medicine catalogue](oral_medicine.csv) | Clinical photographs, mucosal lesions, referral triage, and related resources |
| Oral pathology and oral cancer | [Oral medicine catalogue](oral_medicine.csv) | Histopathology, cytology, clinical images, and head-and-neck cohorts; check anatomical subsite and patient grouping |
| Oral and maxillofacial radiology | [Dental imaging catalogue](dental_imaging.csv) | Panoramic radiographs, periapicals, CBCT, annotations, and segmentation resources |
| Endodontics | [Dental imaging catalogue](dental_imaging.csv) | Periapical radiographs for periapical-lesion classification; further dedicated endodontic resources need discovery |
| Periodontology | [Dental imaging catalogue](dental_imaging.csv) | Panoramic periodontal annotations and a restricted keypoint/object-detection dataset |
| Implant dentistry | [Dental imaging catalogue](dental_imaging.csv) and [digital dentistry catalogue](digital_dentistry.csv) | Some multimodal and panoramic resources support implant research; no implant-only dataset is verified in this initial snapshot |
| Prosthodontics | [Digital dentistry catalogue](digital_dentistry.csv) | Intraoral scans, paired CBCT/oral scans, and jaw-motion resources; access terms vary |
| Restorative and operative dentistry | [Dental imaging catalogue](dental_imaging.csv) | Panoramic disease and dental-structure labels; dedicated caries cohorts need further curation |
| Orthodontics | [Digital dentistry catalogue](digital_dentistry.csv) | General dental scans and facial resources may support methods development; orthodontic labels/cohorts need further curation |
| Pediatric dentistry | [Clinical reasoning benchmarks](clinical_reasoning_benchmarks.csv) | Broad dental knowledge benchmarks may include pediatric questions; no dedicated pediatric patient-imaging cohort verified yet |
| Oral surgery and implant planning | [Dental imaging catalogue](dental_imaging.csv) | CBCT anatomy, missing-tooth annotations, and maxillofacial structures |
| Dental education and knowledge assessment | [Clinical reasoning benchmarks](clinical_reasoning_benchmarks.csv) | DentalBench and GlobalDentBench include dental QA/clinical reasoning; no student-performance cohort verified |
| Digital dentistry and dental materials | [Digital dentistry catalogue](digital_dentistry.csv) | Intraoral scans, facial/4D data used in virtual-patient work, and jaw tracking |
| General dentistry and multimodal research | [Dental imaging catalogue](dental_imaging.csv) and [clinical reasoning benchmarks](clinical_reasoning_benchmarks.csv) | Mixed-modality datasets, clinical reasoning benchmarks, and controlled-access cohorts |

## Coverage rule

“Not yet verified” means the maintainers have not confirmed a primary source and usable metadata for that specialty. It does not mean no dataset exists. Please suggest candidate sources through an issue or pull request.
